Saptarshi
Sinha

Final-year Ph.D. student in computer vision at the University of Bristol.

I work on long-form video and 3D scene understanding, mostly from an egocentric view. I'm supervised by Prof. Dima Damen in the School of Computer Science, and part of MaVi and ViLab.

From 2018 to 2022 I was an AI researcher at Hitachi's Central Research Laboratory in Tokyo, working on long-tailed and activity recognition. Before that, I completed my MTech at IIT Bombay with Prof. Subhasis Chaudhuri.

Barcelona, Spain

May 2026

Four days in Catalonia, taking in Gaudí’s Barcelona from Casa Batlló and Park Güell to the Sagrada Família, a morning among the jagged peaks of Montserrat, and a day out to Girona’s old town and the coves of Tossa de Mar on the Costa Brava.

Annecy, France

May 2026

A weekend in the French Alps during my research visit to Naver Labs Europe in Grenoble. Canals winding through the old town, the twelfth-century Palais de l’Isle, and the turquoise water of Lake Annecy.

Grenoble, France

March–April 2026

Home during my research visit to Naver Labs Europe. Grenoble sits where three mountain ranges meet, with wide views from the Bastille over the Isère and a spring walk to the waterfalls and caves of the Cuves de Sassenage.

Paris, France

April 2026

Two days in Paris, with the Louvre and the Eiffel Tower on the first, then Île de la Cité, from the stained glass of Sainte-Chapelle to the restored Notre-Dame, and on to the Arc de Triomphe.

Geneva, Switzerland

April 2026

A weekend based in Geneva, with the Jet d’Eau on the lakefront, a day up at Glacier 3000 crossing the Peak Walk (a suspension bridge strung between two summits), and an afternoon by the lake in Montreux.

Istanbul & Troy, Türkiye

December 2025 – January 2026

The last leg of the trip took in the Blue Mosque at dusk, a day out to the ruins of Troy and the wooden horse at Çanakkale, New Year’s Eve in Taksim, and New Year’s Day in the Basilica Cistern and at Topkapı Palace, dusted with snow.

Antalya & Pamukkale, Türkiye

December 2025

On from Cappadocia to the southwest, first to the white travertine terraces of Pamukkale and the ruins of ancient Hierapolis above them, then to Antalya, with Hadrian’s Gate lit up at night, the Düden Waterfalls, and sunrise over the sea.

Göreme, Türkiye

December 2025

Christmas in Cappadocia, with hundreds of hot-air balloons rising over the valleys at dawn, an afternoon among the fairy chimneys and the rock castle of Uçhisar, and fresh snow on the last morning.

Glasgow & Edinburgh, Scotland

August 2025

Five days in Scotland, starting at the cloisters of the University of Glasgow. Two days out in the Highlands took me through Glencoe, past Loch Ness and to Glenfinnan as the steam train crossed the viaduct, and in Edinburgh’s Old Town I found the Thistle Chapel in St Giles’ and the colours of Victoria Street.

Around England

2022–2025

Places closer to home, including the Jurassic Coast in Dorset from Durdle Door to Lulworth Cove, Bath Abbey, the Radcliffe Camera in Oxford, a summer day in London, and Hogwarts in the snow at the Warner Bros. Studio Tour.

Hanoi, Ha Long & Ninh Binh, Vietnam

December 2024

Six days in northern Vietnam, taking in Hoan Kiem Lake in Hanoi by night, a day on Ha Long Bay with the climb up Ti Top Island and the caverns of Sung Sot, and a rowing-boat ride through the karst landscape of Tam Coc in Ninh Binh, from bright afternoon into evening haze.

Tokyo & Himeji, Japan

2018–2022

Home for four years while I worked at Hitachi in Tokyo, from Tokyo Tower and the Skytree to Bentendo Hall on Shinobazu Pond and the city in cherry-blossom season. I also climbed Mt Fuji to watch dawn break above the clouds, and took a trip to Himeji Castle.

Seoul, South Korea

October 2019

In Seoul for ICCV 2019 at COEX, with the Gangnam Style statue outside, the towering shelves of the Starfield Library inside, and Halloween in full swing on the streets at night.
