Saptarshi
Sinha

Ph.D. student in computer vision at the University of Bristol, working on long-term video understanding.

I'm in my final year, supervised by Prof. Dima Damen in the School of Computer Science, and part of MaVi and ViLab. My work looks at how models can follow what happens across long egocentric videos: tracking objects over time, counting repetitions, and recognising rare actions.

From 2018 to 2022 I was an AI researcher at Hitachi's Central Research Laboratory in Tokyo, working on long-tailed and activity recognition, which led to three granted Japan patents. Before that, I completed my MTech at IIT Bombay with Prof. Subhasis Chaudhuri. My Ph.D. is funded by an EPSRC Doctoral Training Studentship.
